## Methodology, Caveats & Citation (`CC BY 4.0`)

- **Scope definitions**: **540K+** refers to the full tracked product catalog on [`/products`](https://earlylore.com/products). The **460,446** market scope in [`pricing-models`](data/pricing-models.json) reflects the measured browse subset carrying website traffic or revenue evidence. Similarly, `248` public browsable niches are listed from the `249`-category taxonomy (excluding `adult`).
- **Website traffic**: Monthly visit estimates come from [Vaneform](https://vaneform.com) and cover website activity (including repeat visits) for each product's latest available observation month; combined category visits sum each site's latest available month. Website visits stay separate from paying seats or revenue.
- **Pricing benchmarks**: Median monthly entry prices measure recorded monthly subscription plans; annual-only commitments, one-time purchases, usage-only billing, and undisclosed plans are separated cleanly. Always check each product page and vendor source before quoting live billing limits.
- **Revenue disclosures**: Disclosed or payment-verified revenue appears only where source evidence exists (**10,000+** catalog products / **11,945** in measured browse scope); absent revenue figures remain undisclosed.

## 中文说明（开放数据集）

本仓库定期从 [EarlyLore](https://earlylore.com/zh/market) 公开 Markdown 端点同步 **CC BY 4.0** 开放基准数据（本次同步日期：**2026-11-23**）：

- **规模口径**：全站追踪目录覆盖 **54 万+** 产品；本仓库统计的可测行情口径（含网站流量估算或收入实证产品）覆盖 **460,446** 款产品、**459,332** 个独立站点，其中 **11,945** 款包含公开或支付验证的收入证据（全站公开收入披露产品共 **10,000+** 款）；首屏 Design DNA 覆盖 **130,984**（**13 万+**）个站点。
- **分类说明**：研究分类体系共设 `249` 个细分赛道，默认公开目录与 `data/categories-249.csv` 展示 **248** 个适龄公开可浏览赛道（排除 `adult` 分类）。
- **核心文件**：`data/pricing-models.csv`（商业模式定价分布）、`data/top-20-niches.csv`（20 个核心开发与 AI 赛道流量与入门中位价）、`data/categories-249.csv`（全部 248 个公开可浏览细分分类目录）、`data/design-dna-styles.csv`（130,984 个站点首屏 Design DNA 视觉风格统计）。
- **统计口径提示**：月访问量为 Vaneform 网站访问估算；分类汇总为各站点最近可观测月份之和；分类月付入门中位价仅统计明确公开的按月订阅入门价，不含纯年付、买断或纯按量计费产品；无收入记录表示未公开披露。
